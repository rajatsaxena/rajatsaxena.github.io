I am a systems neuroscientist interested in uncovering the neural circuit mechanisms that enable continual learning, memory consolidation, and cognitive flexibility. Specifically, I seek to understand how the brain continually incorporates new experiences, transfers prior knowledge to new situations, and coordinates hippocampus–neocortex interactions to support learning and memory across multiple timescales. To address these questions, I combine large-scale electrophysiology, two-photon calcium imaging, chemogenetics, behavioral experiments, and computational modeling.

I am currently an [EMBO](https://www.embo.org/funding/fellowships-grants-and-career-support/postdoctoral-fellowships/) Postdoctoral Fellow in the [Moser Group](https://www.ntnu.edu/kavli/moser-group#/view/about) at the Kavli Institute for Systems Neuroscience, where I investigate the neural mechanisms underlying left–right alternating theta sweeps in the entorhinal–hippocampal network. My goal is to determine how coordinated activity across these circuits supports rapid and flexible memory encoding, providing mechanistic insights into how the brain learns from experience and consolidates memories.

I received my PhD from UC Irvine in the [McNaughton lab](https://sites.uci.edu/mcnaughtonlab/), where I studied the neural and computational mechanisms underlying continual learning in biological and artificial systems (see [review paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.07295v3)). During my PhD, I developed [SWIL](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2115229119), a biologically inspired deep learning algorithm that enables rapid, data-efficient learning of new information. I also investigated how enriched experiences reshape cortical circuit organization and population coding [EE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12425001/).

Before joining UC Irvine, I was a Research Assistant in the [Deshmukh Lab](https://snu.edu.in/faculty/sachin-deshmukh/) at IISc Bangalore, where I studied the neural correlates of spatial navigation and memory. During this time, I developed [camera systems](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/full/10.1152/jn.00215.2018) for tracking rodents in large spaces. I received a bachelor's degree in Computer Science & Information Systems from BITS Pilani, India.
